# Stanage VS Challenge: descent options (full list, pre-filter)

Extracted from the Eastern Grit topo arrows and route-text notes (all 65
photographed spreads, pages 134-267), cross-checked against UKC crag-table
positions. Circuit direction: Popular End toward Stanage End, i.e. topo-left
(NW) = ahead, topo-right (SE) = behind. "Serves" = challenge route numbers.

Rule applied downstream: no Diff/VDiff downclimbs (see excluded list).
Filter this list, then the survivors get baked into transition rows.
